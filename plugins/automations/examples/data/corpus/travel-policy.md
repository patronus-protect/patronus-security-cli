# Business travel policy

Employees book flights and trains through the internal travel portal at least
14 days before departure. Economy class is the default for flights under six
hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Rail travel is preferred
for trips under four hours. Hotel bookings must stay within the city rate list
published by Finance each January.
