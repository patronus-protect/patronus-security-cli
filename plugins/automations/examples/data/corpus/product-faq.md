# Product FAQ — international offices

Our sensor line “Nordlicht” ships from Zürich, København and São Paulo.
Japanese packaging reads 北極光センサー; the Korean manual title is 노르트리히트 센서.
Temperature range: −20 °C to +60 °C. Accessories are marked with ✓ when they are
compatible with firmware ≥ 2.4. Order codes look like {"sku": "NL-204", "qty": 3}
in the partner portal export.
