# Expense reimbursement

Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase. Every item above 25 EUR
needs a receipt photo. Meal allowances follow the per-diem table: 28 EUR for a
full day and 14 EUR for a partial day. Reimbursements are paid with the next
monthly payroll run after the manager approves the report.
