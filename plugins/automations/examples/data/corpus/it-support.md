# IT support

Open a ticket in the helpdesk portal for hardware problems. Laptops are replaced
every four years or after a confirmed hardware defect. Loaner laptops are
available at the IT desk on the second floor. Password resets are handled
through self-service; the helpdesk never asks for your password.
